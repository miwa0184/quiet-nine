Privacy: Quiet Nine

Quiet Nine runs no servers of its own, and the app sends no information about you to us.

  • No account of our own and no sign-in of our own.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no frameworks from companies other than Apple.
  • Your puzzles, your history and your settings are stored on your phone, in the app's own storage. We cannot see them, and the app sends them nowhere.
  • The order in which you fill in the cells is saved for each solved puzzle and used to draw your work. It is stored on your phone and is sent nowhere.
  • The app does not ask for access to your location, your contacts, your camera or your microphone, and it never reads your pictures. The only permission the app asks iOS for is to add pictures to Photos, and only if you choose Save Image (see Sharing below).

One optional service from Apple uses the network: Game Center, for Compete

  • You can open the Compete tab without joining. As long as you have not joined, the app never asks for a Game Center sign-in by itself: that happens only when you tap Sign in to Game Center or Join. When you are signed in, the app reads the leaderboards from Apple and shows them: the top players' Game Center names, placements and results, plus your own entry and the number of players. The app does not store the leaderboards.
  • No result is sent until you yourself join Global Daily. After that, results are sent, under your Game Center name, to three leaderboards that Apple runs: your time on each day's puzzle (in whole seconds), the points for the week list and, for the month list (four weeks), the number of days' puzzles solved and the total time. Each result comes with a number showing which day, or which days, it applies to (for the day list also which counting rule was used). Other players can see your Game Center name, your placement and your result on the leaderboards.
  • You can leave Global Daily whenever you like. No more results are sent after that. It does not remove a result that has already been sent.
  • The app stores on your phone a receipt for each result, your choice to take part, and the ID that Game Center gives your account in this app. The ID is used only on the phone, to keep track of who joined, and the app does not send it on. Once you have joined, the app tries to sign you in to Game Center in the background and checks whether the phone has a network connection, to try again with a result that is waiting. The network check sends nothing.

Apple handles that information under Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive it on any server of ours.

Sharing

  • When you share a work, a picture or a link, it goes where you choose in the share sheet. The app sends nothing itself.
  • In the share sheet for a picture you can choose Save Image. The first time, iOS asks whether Quiet Nine may add pictures to Photos, with the text “Quiet Nine saves the picture you share to Photos.” The permission is only for adding, and the app never reads your pictures.
  • Contact in the app is a link to our email address, and Privacy Policy is a link to this page. The app sends nothing itself.

Delete, leave and withdraw consent

On your phone

Open Settings › Your data › Reset all progress. It resets every board, your saved puzzles, your works and your pictures, your recommended level and your Daily record of kept editions. What you have shown in Learn and your settings stay, and so do your choice to take part in Global Daily and the receipts for results already sent.

What the app has saved is in the app's own storage on the phone. If you delete the app from the phone, it is erased. A backup of the phone, if you use one, may contain the app's data.

Global Daily

You join Global Daily by tapping Join yourself, and you withdraw by leaving: open the Compete tab, tap ⓘ and choose Leave Global Daily. No more results are sent after that. “Reset all progress” does not leave Global Daily.

A result that has already been sent is held by Apple. The app has no function for removing it, and leaving Global Daily does not remove it.
