Quiet Nine: support

Here are answers to what people ask most, and an address if you need more help.

Contact

Write to support.quiet.nine@gmail.com. The same address is in the app under Settings › About › About Quiet Nine › Contact.

Please tell us which iPhone and which iOS version you have, which version of Quiet Nine it concerns (Settings › About › Version) and what happened. Never write your password.

You open Settings with the gear icon at the top right of the Play tab.

Frequently asked questions

What is today's puzzle?

A new puzzle arrives every day, and it is the same for everyone. Today's puzzle changes at 23:00 UTC. In Sweden that is midnight (00:00) in winter and 01:00 in summer.

The difficulty varies through the week: Medium on Monday and Tuesday, Hard on Wednesday and Thursday, Expert on Friday and Sunday, and Insane on Saturday, the hardest day of the week.

How does a hint work?

Tap Hint. It comes in three steps: Where, Why and Answer. You enter the digit yourself: a hint never fills in a cell for you.

Help is recorded honestly: a puzzle you solved with a hint is not counted as solved without a hint.

The Fill in the rest button is something else. It appears when 20 cells or fewer are empty. When you tap it, the app fills in the cells if all of them can be filled in using singles alone. If they cannot, nothing is written, and the tap counts as a check, that is, as help. The setting Fill in automatically is off from the start.

What do “Check”, “Go back to where it was right” and “Check against solution” mean?

“Check” tells you whether the digits you have written match the solution, never where anything is wrong.

“Go back to where it was right” takes the board back to the latest position where every digit you had written was right.

“Check against solution” is a setting (Settings › Gameplay) that marks digits you have written that do not match.

All three count as help: a puzzle where you have used them is not counted as solved without help.

How do I join Global Daily?

Open the Compete tab, choose Today and tap Join. No result is sent until you join. If you are not signed in to Game Center, the app asks you to sign in when you join.

A puzzle you solved before you joined is not handed in. You take part from the next Today's puzzle you solve. Only your first attempt at Today's puzzle counts, and only if you play it as Today's puzzle.

What do the lists show?

Compete has three lists: Today, Week and Month. Today ranks players by their time on Today's puzzle. Week counts points: 100 points for each Today's puzzle solved, plus a time bonus of 60 minus the whole minutes it took, never below 0. Month covers four weeks and counts the most Today's puzzles solved; when the counts are equal, the shorter total time decides.

How is the time counted?

The time is active time: the clock runs only while the puzzle is on screen, and stops when you leave the puzzle or the app. Help adds time in the list: 1 minute for a hint, 30 seconds for Check and 1 minute for Go back. Undo adds no time.

If you make moves in Today's puzzle while “Check against solution” is on, you get no placement in Global Daily.

My time has not been sent

If you are not signed in to Game Center, the result waits on your phone: on Compete you can tap Sign in to Game Center. The result is sent when Game Center answers, as long as the edition has not closed. If the edition closes before it could be sent, you get no placement.

How do I leave Global Daily?

Open the Compete tab, tap ⓘ at the top right and choose Leave Global Daily. No more results are sent after that, not even one that was waiting. Leaving does not remove a result that has already been sent.

What is Picture by picture?

Picture by picture is a picture made of 81 tiles. Every puzzle you solve for the first time lays tiles on the picture, more of them the harder the puzzle is. When all the tiles are laid, the picture stands whole.

You have ten days from the first tile. If you do not finish in time, the picture is saved as it is and stays under Your pictures, and the next puzzle starts a new picture. All pictures are open.

How do I save a picture to Photos?

When you share a work or a finished picture (Share the work, Share the picture) you can choose Save Image in the share sheet. The first time, iOS asks whether Quiet Nine may add pictures to Photos, with the text “Quiet Nine saves the picture you share to Photos.” The app never reads your pictures.

Where are my puzzles stored? Can I move them to a new phone?

Your puzzles and history are stored on your phone, in the app's own storage. Quiet Nine has no sync between devices of its own, so the app does not move them for you. A backup of the phone, if you use one, may contain the app's data.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings › Your data › Reset all progress. It resets every board, your saved puzzles, your works and your pictures, your recommended level and your Daily record of kept editions. What you have shown in Learn and your settings stay. To remove everything the app has saved on the phone, delete the app.

“Reset all progress” does not leave Global Daily. Your choice to take part and the receipts for results already sent also stay on the phone, and a result that has already been sent is not removed. Read more on the Privacy page.

Does Quiet Nine send notifications, show ads or need an account?

No. Quiet Nine sends no notifications, shows no ads and has no account of its own. Compete uses your Game Center account, which is Apple's.

Which phones and which languages?

Quiet Nine is for iPhone and needs iOS 18 or later. The app comes in Swedish and English. You choose the language under Settings › Language, or let the app follow iOS.

Accessibility

Quiet Nine adapts to the text size you have chosen in iOS, but some parts, such as the number keys, have a largest size. The app works in light and dark mode. If something does not work with an assistive feature you use, write to us and describe what happens.
